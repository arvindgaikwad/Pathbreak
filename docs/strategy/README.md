## Important interpretation rule

Public store pages expose install bands, ratings, reviews, chart positions, and purchase offers. They do not expose monthly active users, daily active users, exact revenue, retention, acquisition cost, or profit. Any number not directly published by a platform is labelled as an estimate, model, or unknown.

Revenue scenarios are planning models, not forecasts. Retention thresholds are internal decision gates, not guarantees or universal benchmarks.

## Current strategic conclusion

Pathbreak should not compete as a smaller copy of Easybrain's Arrow Puzzle. Its position should be:

> A fair, readable, calming directional-path puzzle with precise touch controls, original chapter mechanics, respectful ads, and dependable offline play.

The defensible assets are the level-production pipeline, solver, difficulty model, original mechanics, touch quality, content cadence, and player trust—not the basic arrow-clearing idea alone.
