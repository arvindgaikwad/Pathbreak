# Pathbreak — Market, Product, Monetization, and Launch Strategy

Status: working strategy pack for production planning. Last researched: 2026-08-06.

This folder contains the business and product documents required to develop Pathbreak as a commercial Android puzzle game rather than only a technical prototype.

## Documents

1. `COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md` — direct and adjacent competitors, public scale signals, feature comparison, gaps, and risks.
2. `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md` — player segments, needs, behaviours, positioning, and research plan.
3. `MONETIZATION_AND_EARNINGS.md` — ethical monetization model, revenue scenarios, costs, and decision gates.
4. `LAUNCH_PLAN.md` — testing, soft launch, store listing, marketing, release, and post-launch cadence.
5. `PRODUCT_AND_RETENTION_PLAN.md` — product pillars, content strategy, retention systems, analytics, and live operations.
6. `RISKS_AND_DECISION_LOG.md` — legal, product, technical, financial, and market risks with mitigations.

## Important interpretation rule

Public store pages expose install bands, ratings, reviews, chart positions, and purchase offers. They do not expose monthly active users, daily active users, exact revenue, retention, acquisition cost, or profit. Any number not directly published by a platform is labelled as an estimate, model, or unknown.

## Current strategic conclusion

Pathbreak should not compete as a smaller copy of Easybrain's Arrow Puzzle. Its position should be:

> A fair, readable, calming directional-path puzzle with precise touch controls, original chapter mechanics, respectful ads, and dependable offline play.

The defensible assets are the level-production pipeline, solver, difficulty model, original mechanics, touch quality, content cadence, and player trust—not the basic arrow-clearing idea alone.
